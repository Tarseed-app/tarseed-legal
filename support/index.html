Deletion Requests

To delete your account or specific app data, you can use the in-app option (Profile → Account Info → Delete Account) or submit a request by email. See the dedicated pages for full details:

  • Account Deletion — permanently close your account and remove all data.
  • Data Deletion — remove specific data without closing your account.
